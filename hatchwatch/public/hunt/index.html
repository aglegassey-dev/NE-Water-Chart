Before you hunt

  • This map is a guide, not legal advice. Boundaries come from state GIS data and may be off by tens of feet, and phone GPS can be off by 15–50+ feet. Respect posted signs and blazes on the ground over anything shown here.
  • Hunting status comes from the Freeport Conservation Trust map Hunting on Freeport Conservation Trust Preserves. Confirm current rules with FCT (207-865-3985, freeportconservationtrust.org). Rules can change during a season.
  • Maine: no hunting on Sundays. Legal shooting time is 30 min before sunrise to 30 min after sunset (the times shown here are calculated).
  • Maine law bars firearm discharge within 100 yards of a residential dwelling without the owner's permission. The safety circles use E911 address points, which can be off from the actual building, and some buildings have no address. Use your own eyes.
  • You need a valid license plus any season, tag and orange-clothing rules from Maine IF&W.
