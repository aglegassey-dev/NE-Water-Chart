How to use

  • ◎ follows your GPS location. The bottom card shows whether you're in a legal zone, the distance to the boundary, and shooting hours.
  • Tap the map to check any spot. Long-press or tap ＋ to drop a waypoint (stand, blind, camera, scrape…) with notes and photos.
  • ● records your track. Layers has presets: Terrain scout shows slope, LiDAR hillshade, contours and creeks. Food / hardwoods shows leaf-off aerial photos plus hardwood forest.
  • Scouting tip: steep ground (orange/red slope) dropping to a creek, a bench on a hillside, or a saddle between ridges, next to hardwood (gray in leaf-off photos) that holds oaks.

Before you hunt

  • This app is a guide, not legal advice. Boundaries come from state GIS data and may be off by tens of feet, and phone GPS can be off by 15–50+ feet. Posted signs and blazes on the ground take priority.
  • Zone status comes from the Freeport Conservation Trust map Hunting on Freeport Conservation Trust Preserves. Confirm current rules with FCT (207-865-3985). Rules can change during a season.
  • Maine: no Sunday hunting. Legal shooting time is 30 min before sunrise to 30 min after sunset (the times shown are calculated). Follow license, tag and orange-clothing rules from Maine IF&W.
  • The 100-yard circles use E911 address points, which can be off from the actual building, and some buildings have no address. Use your eyes.
  • The forest layers are satellite/model estimates (30 m and 250 m), so use them to decide where to look. Confirm oaks on foot.
